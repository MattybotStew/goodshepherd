Making Home Happen

Overview

At Good Shepherd Manor, home means safety and belonging, dignity and compassion. It means familiar routines, meaningful relationships, and knowing someone will notice when you are struggling and celebrate when you succeed.

We are honored to create that sense of home for the men we serve and grateful for the trust their families place in us.

Placeholder photo — homes on the Momence campus.

Personalized Support, Everyday Connection

All thirteen of Good Shepherd Manor’s residential homes operate as Community Integrated Living Arrangements, or CILAs. This model emphasizes individualized support, personal choice, and community involvement while providing the assistance each man needs in daily life.

Each home offers a family-like atmosphere, with residents gathering for family-style breakfasts and dinners each day. With support suited to their abilities, the men help with cooking, cleaning, vacuuming, laundry, and other household responsibilities. These everyday activities build practical skills, encourage independence, and nurture pride in their shared home.

Dedicated staff provide support around the clock, helping each man build independence, maintain relationships, and participate in activities he enjoys. Each person’s needs, preferences, routines, and compatibility with housemates help guide a comfortable and welcoming home environment.

Shared meals, outings, celebrations, and opportunities for prayer bring people together. Whether enjoying a quiet evening with housemates or welcoming families for special events, these experiences strengthen the connections that make GSM home.

Present, Involved Leadership

Quality care begins with listening and being present. Through Shepherd’s Rounds, named in tribute to the Brothers who first stewarded this community, members of our senior leadership team visit homes each week to connect with the men and staff.

These visits provide opportunities to hear concerns, recognize what is working well, and address challenges promptly. They strengthen relationships and keep leadership connected to everyday life in our homes.

Supporting the People Who Care

The people who work in our homes are central to the quality of life the men experience. Staff training, including Safety Care, helps team members respond compassionately, de-escalate challenging situations, and support everyone’s safety.

Developing leaders from within our team brings firsthand knowledge of residential life into supervisory roles. Clear documentation and communication tools, including the GSM Portal, help staff share information and provide consistent support across homes.

Comfort, Safety, and Stability

A dependable environment helps the men feel comfortable and confident in their daily lives. Maintaining heating, cooling, power, and lighting is an essential part of that commitment. Preventive maintenance and thoughtful upgrades help reduce unexpected disruptions and preserve familiar routines.

Improvements to boilers and heating and cooling equipment support comfort throughout the seasons. Whole-home generators, made possible through the generosity of families and donors, help residents remain in their homes during power outages. Planning for maintenance and eventual replacement helps preserve that reliability.

Lighting along walkways, parking areas, and circle drives improves visibility after dark, supporting movement across campus and participation in activities.

Staying Connected

An upgraded communication system, radios, and campus technology help residential staff coordinate with nursing, supervisors, and support services during daily activities and emergencies. Maintaining these systems helps staff share information and respond when needs arise.

Growing with the Men We Serve

Since our founding in 1971, Good Shepherd Manor has adapted to meet the changing needs of the men entrusted to our care. The Brothers and founding families helped establish a community that recognized the abilities, dignity, and humanity of people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

In the 1990s, GSM began moving away from dormitory-style living into Intermediate Care Facility homes and later opened smaller CILA homes. These changes supported greater privacy, personal choice, and opportunities for connection.

Making Home Happen

Making home happen is the everyday work of Good Shepherd Manor. It is found in trusted relationships, shared laughter, thoughtful support, and quiet moments of kindness—and in the attention that keeps each home running smoothly.

Dedicated staff, supportive families, generous donors, and community partners make this work possible. Together, they sustain our steadfast mission: providing high-quality, compassionate care to men with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
